This is a pretty wasteful way to find out where to punch and tap the Z axis motor mount holes, but it does work, and does not require the use of a tape measure
or much thinking, meaning it is a reliable way for novice people to find the center holes. If you are more advanced measuring out the center point and mounting
based on that would be a more cost-saving measure, but that is on you.

This was designed to fit straight on a 256 x 256 bed, although slanted on a 45 degree angle it can fit within 208.6 x 208.6. 

Print both supportless.

The final cost is an estimated $1.
That is cheap enough to justify printing this rather than spend 15 minutes finding and marking the center, but again the choice is given.
I do believe there is a best of both worlds scenario that I can adopt in the future, but that will require future revision.
